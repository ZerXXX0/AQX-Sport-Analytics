METRIC DEFINITIONS
FG%Makes ÷ Attempts
eFG%(Makes + 0.5 × Three-Point Makes) ÷ Attempts
Points / AttemptObserved points from field goals only; excludes free throws
ClutchQuarter ≥4, time ≤5:00 remaining, absolute score margin ≤5 before shot
Wilson Interval95% binomial confidence interval for make rate; 30 attempts is a display threshold only
MODEL DETAILS

Chronological 70/15/15 split by date; games stay together. Imputation and scaling fit training data only. No outcome, final score, shot-result description, or post-shot score is used as a feature.

LIMITATIONS
  • •No defender distance or shot clock data available
  • •Estimates are observational associations, not causal recommendations
  • •Data is historical; app does not provide live NBA results
  • •One season (2023/24 regular season) only
  • •Model uncertainty and changing player roles limit recommendations
  • •Score context assumes initial score 0–0; source corrections and event ordering are limitations
